NIGHTJAR

Slip through the manor unseen. Learn the patrols. Use the dark.
Photograph each floor's ledger — then walk out the EXIT like you were never there.

  • Vision cones — guards see you in their cone. Distance, lamplight and sneaking change how fast they spot you.
  • Shadows hide you — dark floor halves detection. Lamplight nearly doubles it. Walls, crates and hedges block sight.
  • Footsteps carry — running feet are heard. Sneak past a guard's ear.
  • Coins distract — click to toss a coin (3 per floor). Guards leave their route to check the clink.
  • Cameras sweep — their red cones feed the house alarm. Fill it and every guard hunts you.
  • GHOST — finish the campaign without ever being seen.
